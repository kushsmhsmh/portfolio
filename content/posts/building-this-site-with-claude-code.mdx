This site started as a single request: a portfolio with a linked blog, built to actually look like *me* rather than a template. I picked three references to anchor the direction — a minimal text-first site, a structured "resume-style" portfolio, and a bold, interactive one. I went with bold and interactive.

## The stack

- **Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript** for routing and static generation.
- **Tailwind CSS v4** for the design system — a small set of color tokens, one accent color, and a type scale built around large display type.
- **Framer Motion** for scroll reveals and the hero intro.
- **MDX**, compiled from local files in `content/posts/`, for posts like this one — no CMS, no database, just files committed to the repo.

## Why real content from day one

Instead of shipping with "Lorem ipsum" placeholders, the experience section, project list, and contact links here are pulled directly from my CV and GitHub. It made the design decisions easier too — you can't tell if a layout works until real text of real lengths is sitting in it.

## What's next

More posts, mostly about the research and ML projects linked on the [projects page](/projects) — what worked, what didn't, and the parts that don't make it into a two-line CV bullet.
